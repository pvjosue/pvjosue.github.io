Interactive projects for bouldering gyms

One modular platform for competition, training and everyday climbing. Start with a focused pilot, then add capabilities as the gym grows.

One platform, three ways to climb

Projection mapping, pose tracking and live data can work together across event days and daily gym use. Expand a project to see its tiers; each tier opens the detailed project proposal.

01 / EVENT DAY

Competition

Turn finals into a clear, engaging show with live results and judging.

Final Show

A scalable visual show, from sponsor moments to interactive wall projection.
  1. Tier 1 · Comp Day Show: Visual Night + SponsorsSmall-wall visuals and sponsor graphics throughout competition day. 1–2 weeks; target budget up to €1,000.
  2. Tier 2 · Interactive Comp ShowLive replays and mapped visuals; budget depends on the wall area covered.

Real-time leaderboard & scoring

Phone-based scoring with results for a venue screen; show integration is optional.
  1. Tier 1 · Side-screen resultsBasic phone scoring and screen display: up to €500 with a venue screen, plus €100 if rental is needed.
  2. Tier 2 · Integrated showUse event scores in the projection and stream graphics, scoped with the Comp Day Show.
Shared foundation Event scores can be used in the show and stream graphics when that integration is selected.
02 / GET BETTER

Training

Help setters and climbers plan, review and improve every session.

Setter & Training Projection Tool

Plan routes and project drills directly onto the wall.
  1. Tier 1 · Route previewPlan hold positions and colors on a tablet, then preview them on the wall.
  2. Tier 2 · Coach-led drillsProject target sequences and movement drills for athletes.
  3. Tier 3 · Shared setter librarySave, compare and reuse route plans across sessions.

Fall Heatmap & Beta Memory

Make common sticking points visible and useful to climbers and setters.
  1. Tier 1 · Crux mapShow where attempts most often stop on a problem.
  2. Tier 2 · Beta memoryReview anonymized movement patterns and successful sequences.
  3. Tier 3 · Setter insightsCompare intended difficulty with how climbers experience a route.

Run Comparison Tool

Compare attempts in sync to see how movement and timing change.
  1. Tier 1 · Personal progressCompare your latest try with an earlier attempt.
  2. Tier 2 · Coach reviewReview synchronized runs with a coach and spot technique differences.
  3. Tier 3 · Team or gym leagueShare consented runs for friendly challenges across climbers or gyms.
New proposal

Movement Feedback Coach

Turn recorded attempts into simple, coach-reviewed feedback on balance, pacing and body position.
  1. Tier 1 · Review clipsSave and annotate key moments after a climb.
  2. Tier 2 · Progress notesTrack coach feedback and personal goals over time.
New proposal

Adaptive Training Circuits

Build repeatable circuits from the gym's routes, with intervals and rest periods projected nearby.
  1. Tier 1 · Coach-built circuitsChoose routes, sequence and timing for a session.
  2. Tier 2 · Session trackingSave completed circuits and adjust future sessions with a coach.
03 / EVERY VISIT

Daily Gym

Give members new reasons to play, explore and come back.

Permanent Interactive Wall

An always-ready projected play zone for members of all ages.
  1. Tier 1 · Single-zone pilotOne autonomous game on a small section of wall.
  2. Tier 2 · Rotating game modesSeasonal challenges and fresh ways to play.
  3. Tier 3 · Member experienceOptional session artwork, QR sharing and a web gallery.

Sound Wall

Let climbers make music as they move across the holds.
  1. Tier 1 · Event modeA short, social experience for community nights and activations.
  2. Tier 2 · Daily sound modeA quieter, always-available sound experience with adjustable volume.
New proposal

Community Challenge Wall

Give the gym a new weekly challenge that members can try at their own pace.
  1. Tier 1 · Weekly challengeDisplay a rotating problem or movement goal and track opt-in attempts.
  2. Tier 2 · Community seasonBuild friendly team and club challenges with a live leaderboard.
New proposal

Route Passport

A simple digital logbook that connects the wall to each climber's progress.
  1. Tier 1 · Scan & saveUse route QR codes to save problems and personal notes.
  2. Tier 2 · Personal historyTrack completed routes and revisit goals across gym visits.

Start small, build together

Projects share calibration, cameras and a data layer where possible. A venue and wall check can identify the right first tier and what can be added later.
