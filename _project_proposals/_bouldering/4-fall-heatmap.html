Training Projection, Fall Heatmap & Beta Memory

Project training drills and turn anonymized attempts into useful route insights.

Length
Permanent
Budget
€6k–13k planning estimate for the combined modules, on top of the Permanent Interactive Wall
Audience
Climbers, setters, coaches and professional athletes

Idea

Use the projected wall both as a training aid and as a way to make climbing attempts useful over time. Coaches can project target holds or movement sequences for drills; attempt data builds up into heatmaps that show where climbers stop on each problem.

Training projection

Coaches project target holds and movement sequences for athletes to follow.

Fall heatmap

Highlight the holds and moves where attempts most often end.

Beta memory

Review anonymized movement patterns and successful sequences to help climbers understand a problem.

Setter feedback

Compare intended difficulty with how climbers experience a route.

Audience

Recreational climbers, setters, coaches and professional athletes.

Tech stack

  • Camera pose tracking and wall calibration from the Permanent Interactive Wall setup
  • Tablet interface for selecting training sequences and reviewing route data
  • Hold map and database of attempts per problem
  • Projection or screen view for drills and heatmaps

Budget

Combined launch partner planning estimate, on top of the Permanent Interactive Wall hardware. Final scope and price depend on the wall dimensions, tracking setup and desired training features.

Requirements & risks

  • Heatmaps need enough attempts to show meaningful patterns.
  • Use anonymized data by default; identify athletes only when they opt in.
  • Projection needs a calibrated view of the wall and should not distract climbers during attempts.

Value

  • Gives coaches a visual way to guide movement drills.
  • Helps climbers and setters understand common cruxes.
  • Provides objective feedback for training and route-setting decisions.
