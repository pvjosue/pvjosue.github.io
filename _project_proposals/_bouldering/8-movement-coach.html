Movement Feedback Coach

Turn recorded attempts into coach-reviewed feedback on balance, pacing and body position.

Delivery time
4–8 weeks
Planning budget
€4k–10k
Audience
Climbers and coaches

Idea

Capture a climb and let a climber or coach mark important moments: a missed foothold, a balance adjustment or a change in pace. The first release is a review tool, not an automated judge or substitute for coaching.

Tier 1 · Review clips

Record an attempt, replay it and add timestamps or notes for a coaching session.

Tier 2 · Progress notes

Save reviewed clips and goals to a climber's timeline, then compare feedback across sessions.

Delivery & budget

Tier 1 · Review clips4–6 weeks · €3k–6k
Tier 2 · Progress notes (including Tier 1)6–8 weeks · €4k–10k

Planning estimate assumes the gym already has camera coverage and a local recording computer. Additional cameras, cloud hosting and ongoing support are separate.

Requirements & privacy

  • Use an existing camera and recording setup where possible.
  • Climbers choose whether a clip is saved; sharing requires explicit consent.
  • Set a retention policy and access controls for saved video.
  • Automated pose feedback can be scoped later, after tracking accuracy is tested on the actual wall.

Value

  • Makes coaching feedback concrete and easy to revisit.
  • Helps climbers notice changes across attempts and sessions.
  • Builds on the Run Comparison Tool and existing camera setup.
