Interactive projects for bouldering gyms

Projection mapping, pose tracking and live data, built in TouchDesigner. Six projects, from a one-day competition show to installations that run all year.

How it works

Every project builds on one modular core: camera calibration, pose tracking, run recording and comparison, wall mapping and a data layer. The finals show is the showcase; the permanent wall is the recurring base; the other ideas are modules that reuse the same hardware.

Pricing

The first projects are offered at launch partner prices, in exchange for photo and video rights and a testimonial. Equipment rental and transport are billed at cost. Every project has a smaller starting version, so you can begin with a pilot and grow from there.
