Interactive projects for bouldering gyms

One modular platform for competition, training and everyday climbing. Start with a focused pilot, then add capabilities as the gym grows.

One platform, three ways to climb

Projection mapping, pose tracking and live data can work together across event days and daily gym use. Expand a project to see its tiers; each tier opens the detailed project proposal.

01 / EVENT DAY

Competition

Turn the final into a clear, engaging show with visuals, live replays and sponsor moments.

Final Show

A scalable show, from sponsor visuals to interactive wall projection.
  1. Tier 1 · Comp Day Show: Visual Night + SponsorsSmall-wall visuals and sponsor graphics for the final. Can integrate with an existing leaderboard and scoring system; a custom build is available for an additional fee.
  2. Tier 2 · Interactive Comp ShowLive replays and mapped visuals. Can integrate with an existing leaderboard and scoring system; a custom build is available for an additional fee.
02 / GET BETTER

Training

Help climbers, trainers and setters, review and improve every session.

Training Projection, Fall Heatmap & Beta Memory

Project training drills and turn anonymized attempts into useful route insights.
  1. Tier 1 · Training projectionProject target holds and movement sequences for coach-led drills.
  2. Tier 2 · Fall heatmapShow where attempts most often stop on a problem.
  3. Tier 3 · Beta memory & setter insightsReview anonymized movement patterns and compare intended difficulty with how climbers experience a route.

Run Comparison Tool

Compare attempts in sync to see how movement and timing change.
  1. Tier 1 · Personal progressCompare your latest try with an earlier attempt.
  2. Tier 2 · Coach reviewReview synchronized runs with a coach and spot technique differences.
  3. Tier 3 · Team or gym leagueShare consented runs for friendly challenges across climbers or gyms.

Setter Tool

Plan routes on a tablet and preview hold placement directly on the wall.
  1. Tier 1 · Route planningPlace holds and assign colors in a simple tablet interface.
  2. Tier 2 · Wall previewProject the planned route onto the bare wall to guide setting and check placements.
  3. Tier 3 · Shared setter librarySave, compare and reuse route plans across sessions.
