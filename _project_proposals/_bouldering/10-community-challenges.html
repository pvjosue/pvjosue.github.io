Community Challenge Wall

A rotating set of friendly challenges that gives members a reason to try something new on every visit.

Delivery time
3–6 weeks
Planning budget
€3k–8k
Audience
Members, friends, clubs and community events

Idea

Put a weekly challenge on a wall display or projected zone. Members can opt in, record a result and see a friendly leaderboard. The challenge can be a route, a movement goal or a team event.

Tier 1 · Weekly challenge

Show one rotating challenge, collect opt-in results and display a simple leaderboard.

Tier 2 · Community season

Run a multi-week season with teams, club events and sponsor branding. Participants can choose a nickname or remain unlisted.

Delivery & budget

Tier 1 · Weekly challenge3–4 weeks · €2k–5k
Tier 2 · Community season (including Tier 1)4–6 weeks · €3k–8k

Planning estimate assumes the gym provides a screen or an existing projection setup. Displays, projector hardware and long-term hosting are separate.

Requirements & privacy

  • Choose how results are verified: staff entry, judge entry or an existing scoring source.
  • Make participation optional and allow nicknames or hidden results.
  • Agree on challenge rotation and moderation before launch.

Value

  • Creates a social activity that works for different skill levels.
  • Supports community nights and sponsor activations.
  • Can share the live results and display layer with competition projects.
