Adaptive Training Circuits

Build repeatable circuits from the gym's routes, with intervals and rest periods projected nearby.

Delivery time
3–6 weeks
Planning budget
€3k–7k
Audience
Coaches, training groups and regular members

Idea

A coach selects routes and orders them into a repeatable session. A tablet or nearby display guides the climber through attempts, work periods and rest without needing a coach to keep time manually.

Tier 1 · Coach-built circuits

Create a circuit from route names, sequence, work time and rest time. Display the current step and timer.

Tier 2 · Session tracking

Save completed sessions and notes so a coach can review consistency and adjust future circuits.

Delivery & budget

Tier 1 · Coach-built circuits3–4 weeks · €2k–4k
Tier 2 · Session tracking (including Tier 1)4–6 weeks · €3k–7k

Planning estimate assumes a simple tablet or screen interface and existing gym Wi-Fi. Projector, display hardware and integration with member accounts are separate.

Requirements

  • Agree on a small set of session formats with coaches for the first release.
  • Route names or IDs need to match the gym's current setting system.
  • Keep the timer and session controls usable from the wall without interrupting the climb.

Value

  • Makes structured training easier to repeat.
  • Gives coaches a reusable session-planning tool.
  • Can grow into personalized training plans after a pilot.
