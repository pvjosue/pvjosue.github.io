Fall Heatmap and Beta Memory

Where do climbers fall? The wall remembers.

Length
Permanent
Budget
€4k–8k (module of Project 2)
Audience
Recreational climbers, setters, coaches and professional athletes

Idea

The system accumulates where climbers fall or stop on each problem over weeks, then projects the heatmap on demand. It shows exactly which moves stop people.

Heatmap on demand

Warm colors on the holds and moves where most attempts end.

Teaching tool

Members see the crux of a problem before trying it.

Training data

Coaches and athletes track recurring failure points and adjust technique and training plans.

Setter feedback

Setters see whether a problem is as hard as planned.

Audience

Recreational climbers, setters, coaches and professional athletes

Length

Permanent module. Data builds up over weeks.

Tech stack

  • Camera pose tracking from the Project 2 setup
