Route Passport

A simple digital logbook that connects the wall to each climber's progress.

Delivery time
3–5 weeks
Planning budget
€2k–6k
Audience
Members and gym staff

Idea

QR codes on routes open a lightweight mobile page where climbers can save a problem and record a personal note. A later tier adds a private history of completed routes and goals.

Tier 1 · Scan & save

Open a route page from its QR code and save it to a simple personal list with notes.

Tier 2 · Personal history

Track attempts, completed routes and goals over time, with an optional account or gym membership connection.

Delivery & budget

Tier 1 · Scan & save3–4 weeks · €2k–4k
Tier 2 · Personal history (including Tier 1)4–5 weeks · €3k–6k

Planning estimate assumes route data can be exported from the gym's setting workflow. QR printing, account-system integration and long-term hosting are separate.

Requirements & privacy

  • Each route needs a stable identifier and basic route information.
  • Start with optional, minimal user data; do not make a public profile the default.
  • Define a clear retention and account-deletion process if personal history is enabled.

Value

  • Makes it easy for members to remember routes they want to revisit.
  • Creates a simple bridge between physical routes and digital gym services.
  • Can later connect to the Community Challenge Wall or training plans.
